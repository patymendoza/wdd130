Peru

Peru is a beautiful country in Latin America with a diverse climate and three regions: the coast, the highlands, and the jungle. It is known for its delicious food, stunning landscapes, and rich history, including the Inca civilization. Peru is home to Machu Picchu, one of the Seven Wonders of the World, and its people are warm and friendly.
